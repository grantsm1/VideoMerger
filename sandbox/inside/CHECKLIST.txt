VIDEOMERGER - CLEAN PC TEST
===========================

This Sandbox is a brand-new copy of Windows: no FFmpeg, no Python.
Everything is thrown away when you close it, except the "Test videos"
folder on the desktop, which is shared with your real PC.

Write down anything that looks wrong, confusing, or slow.


1. FIRST LAUNCH
   [ ] Double-click VideoMerger.exe on the desktop.
   [ ] Expect "Windows protected your PC" (the app isn't code-signed yet).
       Click "More info" -> "Run anyway". Your friends will see this too.
   [ ] The window opens (may take ~5-10 seconds the first time).
   [ ] An "FFmpeg needed" box appears. Click "Download".
   [ ] The progress bar fills (about 110 MB), then "FFmpeg is ready". Click OK.

2. LOSSLESS MERGE (matching clips)
   [ ] Click "Add files...", open "Test videos" on the desktop,
       select "01 Beach day" and "02 Sunset", click Open.
   [ ] The message under "Advanced" says the videos match and will be
       joined without re-encoding.
   [ ] Click Merge. It should finish in a second or two.
   [ ] Click "Show in folder" and play merged.mp4:
       Beach day (440 Hz tone) then Sunset (higher tone), 11 seconds total.

3. MIXED MERGE (everything different)
   [ ] Click "Clear". Drag ALL the videos from the "Test videos" folder
       onto the list (drag-and-drop from File Explorer).
   [ ] "Not a video.mp4" is refused with a clear message; the others are added.
   [ ] Drag "05 Silent screen recording" to the top of the list.
   [ ] The message says the videos will be re-encoded and explains why.
   [ ] Click Merge. Watch the progress bar and "time left".
   [ ] Play the result: order is 05, 01, 02, 03, 04. The vertical phone clip
       has black bars at the sides, nothing is stretched, sound stays in sync,
       and 05 is silent.

4. CANCEL
   [ ] Start the same merge again (say "Yes" to replace the file),
       then click Cancel halfway.
   [ ] It stops, says "Cancelled", and no half-finished file is left behind.

5. SECOND LAUNCH
   [ ] Close VideoMerger and open it again.
   [ ] It does NOT ask to download FFmpeg again.
   [ ] Help -> About shows the FFmpeg version and where it came from.

When you're done, close the Sandbox window and tell Claude how it went.
Claude can check the merged videos in the "Test videos" folder.
